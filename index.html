How to build a simple skincare routine

Start with a gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and broad-spectrum sunscreen in the morning. Introduce new active ingredients slowly, one at a time, and follow each product's directions.

What to check before buying skincare

Read the ingredient list, choose products suited to your needs, check seller details, and avoid expecting overnight results.
